Split is a speedcubing timer and algorithm trainer that works completely offline. No accounts, no ads, no tracking, and the app asks for no permissions at all.

<b>TIMER</b>
• Hold-to-start timer for touch or keyboard
• Optional 15-second inspection with beeps at 8 and 12 seconds, and the +2 / DNF rules
• Random-state 3x3 scrambles
• +2 and DNF penalties, best, ao5, ao12, and a history with a progress chart
• Separate sessions for different practice
• Import your old solves from csTimer, CSV files or a plain list of times

<b>TRAIN</b>
• PLL (21 cases), OLL (57), F2L (41 cases in each of the four slots) and ZBLL (import your own algorithms)
• Case-specific scrambles with a random AUF, so you meet each case from different angles
• Recognition time and execution time measured separately, so you know what to work on
• Spaced repetition (FSRS) schedules each case just before you would forget it. It can also learn from your own history how fast you forget
• Case diagrams generated from the algorithm itself
• Several algorithms per case: pick your default or add your own, each one checked to really solve the case
• Drills: cross only, 2-look OLL, 2-look PLL and BLD memo timing

<b>MAKE IT YOURS</b>
• Rebindable keyboard shortcuts and hideable panels
• Three themes, including high contrast, and adjustable text size
• Export and restore backups. Everything stays on your device
