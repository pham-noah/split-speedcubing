# Privacy policy for Split

*Last updated: 3 November 2026*

**Split collects no personal data.** There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The only
network request the app can make is the optional update check described below.

## What the app stores, and where

Split stores your solves, training progress, settings and any algorithms you add **only on your own device**, in the app's private storage (on Android and in the desktop app) or in your browser's storage (in the web version). Nothing is uploaded to any server, and the developer cannot see it.

## Permissions

- **Android:** Split requests **no permissions**. It does not request internet access, and Android's cloud auto-backup is switched off for it, so your data is not copied to Google Drive or anywhere else. Because the app cannot reach the network, it does not offer the update check; updates arrive through the store you installed it from.
- **Desktop:** the app makes no network connections unless you turn on the update check (off by default).
- **Web version:** the page is delivered by whoever hosts it, and that host may keep ordinary server logs (for example your IP address) as for any website. This is outside the app's control. After the first visit the web version works offline and makes no further requests.

## Files you choose to export or import

When you press *Export backup*, Split saves a file to a location you pick. When you *Import* or *Restore*, Split reads only the file you select, on your device. Nobody else receives these files unless you share them yourself.

## Updates

The desktop app can check once for a newer release, and only if you turn it on in *Settings > About*. It sends one
request to `api.github.com` for the newest published release and nothing else: no device id, no usage data, no
identifier of any kind, and the reply is used only to show you a version number and a link. It is off by default,
it never downloads or installs anything, and turning it off stops it completely. Your solves, progress and settings
are never part of that request.

## Third parties

Split contains no third-party services, SDKs, analytics or advertising code. The open-source libraries it uses are listed in `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md` and all run locally.

## Deleting your data

Use *Settings > Erase all data* to delete everything the app holds, or uninstall the app (or clear the site data in your browser). Because nothing is stored elsewhere, that removes it completely. Make a backup first if you want to keep it.

## Children

Split does not collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published with the app and the date above updated.
